CEIT Metaverse Collaborates with Khonkhean University for Developing KKU Metaverse

The CEIT Metaverse team has partnered with Khonkhean University to co-develop the KKU Metaverse, an immersive virtual campus environment designed to bring students, faculty, and researchers together in a shared digital space.

The collaboration combines our team's expertise in real-time 3D engines, interactive world building, and VR experiences with Khonkhean University's academic resources to create a platform for virtual lectures, campus tours, and collaborative research spaces.

This partnership marks an important milestone for CEIT Metaverse, extending our reach beyond game development into education-focused virtual world projects. Further updates on development progress and launch timelines will be shared as the project advances.
