Field Survey

Smart Network Coverage Survey

Mitr Lao Sugar needed to verify LoRaWAN connectivity across thousands of hectares in Savannakhet Province before committing to a full-scale sensor deployment for their agricultural operations.

Our team conducted an extensive field-based coverage survey, mapping signal strength and gateway range across varied terrain to identify optimal gateway placement and confirm reliable long-range connectivity.

The survey results gave Mitr Lao Sugar the confidence to proceed with a wide-area IoT deployment, backed by data-driven coverage maps and recommended infrastructure placement.
